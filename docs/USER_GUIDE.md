# User guide

## Prepare content

1. Open the workspace and add an image, video or audio file from your device.
2. Select an image or video in Studio. Choose portrait, square or landscape output.
3. Adjust the crop; trim video where needed; enter your branding and timed captions manually.
4. Preview the result, then save a draft or export the prepared file.
5. Inspect the downloaded output before posting it through the destination platform.

Images export as PNG. Video uses the browser's supported MediaRecorder format, commonly WebM. Platform uploads may require conversion to another codec. Exports run in real time and should remain in a visible tab. Audio files can be stored and downloaded; there is no audio transcription workflow.

## Review and reuse

Draft versions preserve preparation choices. Approvals provide a personal checklist; they do not invite a reviewer or enforce team permissions. Workflows reuse selected formats and destinations and can produce a ZIP content kit. Preparing a destination does not publish to that account.

## Research and plan

Save a source URL and your observations. Enter public reaction, comment and follower figures only when available. Missing metrics are excluded from calculated comparisons. These observations do not establish private reach, conversions or competitor account access.

Create a publication plan and export its calendar reminder. Import the `.ics` file into your calendar and verify the displayed local time. Reminders do not execute a server-side publishing job.

## Connect an account

Google Drive can import supported authorised media. YouTube and Twitch can list your authorised account's uploads/videos. The connection must be enabled in the deployment and authorised by you before these operations work. Use Disconnect to revoke or clear access. Other platform cards are not active account integrations in this repository.

## Keep your work

Use Backups to export a full workspace ZIP with original media. Store it privately alongside your original files. Sign-in does not synchronise content across devices, and clearing browser data removes the local workspace.
